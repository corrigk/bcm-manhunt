The game

  • 7:45 PM to 9:00 PM. Four leaders (Kevin, Owen, Gabe, Jack) each roam an assigned quadrant of the ~92-acre campus game area.
  • Capture as many leaders as possible, as quickly as possible. Chase them in any order and switch targets any time.
  • Leaders send a photo of their location every 5 minutes and may keep moving.

Capturing

  • Touch the leader and receive their codeword.
  • A majority of your team must be within arm's reach (5 people needs 3).
  • Each leader can be captured once per team, and stays active for other teams.
  • Your timer resets the instant you receive the correct codeword.

Movement

  • No sharing information or collaborating with other teams.
  • Stay together. The whole team must be in the same quadrant at all times.
  • You can spread out within a quadrant, but a majority must converge for a capture.
  • Walking and running only. No bikes, scooters, cars.

Endgame

  • At 8:55 PM all leaders head back to Newman Hall and remain capturable.
  • A leader is safe once they cross the designated street in front of St. Thomas Aquinas / Newman.
  • Be back at Newman Hall by 9:00 PM. Every second late costs 2 points.

Scoring

Scoring is based on the time between captures, not the clock time. The first interval starts at 7:45 PM and each capture resets it. Leader identity does not matter.

Capture = 6,000,000 / (600 + seconds since previous capture), rounded.

Final = sum of captures + 1,000 if all four caught − 2 × seconds late

Three fast captures can beat four slow ones. Every capture is always worth something, so keep hunting.
